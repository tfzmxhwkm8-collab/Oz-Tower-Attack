Oz Tower Attack

Defend the towers. Enemies come from the right and attack the rightmost tower first (C → B → A).

  • Tap/click anywhere to fire a volley (all living archers shoot).
  • Arrows arc — aim higher to hit enemies farther back.
  • Earn 1/2/3 crowns depending on how many arrows hit during that volley.
  • Log (60s) kills enemies on the path. Oil (45s) burns enemies near the rightmost tower.
  • Rocks (30s) throws 3 rocks that land in the back 1/4 and kill on impact.
  • Get 1.5× damage after 3 consecutive 3-hit volleys.
  • Keyboard: Space/Enter fires, 1=Log, 2=Oil, 3=Rocks.
  • Drag after tapping to adjust aim.
Sound turns on after you press Start (required on mobile).
